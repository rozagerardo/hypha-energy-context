# AGENTS.md — `planning/`

> Read the root [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) first for the global frame.

## What this directory is

`planning/` holds the near-term "what are we working toward / what is active now" view. Less granular
than `progress/members/`: priorities, active tracks, what is parked.

## What's here

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `current.md` | Near-term priorities and active tracks, with links to the board and the agreement. |
| `issue-guidelines.md` | Conventions observed in the Hypha-Energy org for issues and the board. Short; grows as conventions are confirmed. |

## Relationship to other sources

- The **GitHub board** (org project #13, "hypha.energy website development") is the source of truth for
  website tickets: https://github.com/orgs/Hypha-Energy/projects/13. `current.md` is a narrative view, not a replacement.
- The **agreement** (`ger-hypha-energy/agreements/`) is the source of truth for Ger's blocks and their
  acceptance criteria.
- Board sync is a workflow: [`../workflow/github-sync.md`](../workflow/github-sync.md), `/henergy-board`.
  **Reads are free; writes ask first.**

## Conventions

- Keep `current.md` aligned with the board and the agreement. Link rather than duplicate.
- Neutral voice.
